## What is IdaCode?

**IdaCode** is an Iranian platform that connects **idea owners and business owners** with **developers**.
Post your idea or problem, developers see it and respond, and together you turn it into a real product.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/IdaCode-ir/.github/main/profile/assets/how-it-works.svg" width="100%" alt="How IdaCode works">
